## Project Overview

Google Sheets project focused on cleaning and organizing quarterly sales data, calculating key measures, and presenting the results in a summary.

## Business question

How can a raw quarterly sales dataset be cleaned, standardized, and summarized into reliable commercial KPIs for executive review?

## Approach

The project focused on preparing the data for analysis and building a concise executive summary. The workflow included:

- Cleaning and standardizing raw sales data
- Normalizing text fields and categories
- Calculating key commercial metrics
- Comparing product, city, and monthly performance
- Creating summary views for decision support

## Key findings

- **Tablet** was the best-selling product by quantity.
- **Monterrey** recorded the highest total sales: **$541,137.36**.
- **November** was the best-performing month, with **$1,027,372.46** in sales.
- The analysis covered **753 transactions**, with total quarterly sales of **$2,944,620.61**.
- No duplicate records were found. The original data contained **10 missing Unit Price values** and **8 missing Total Sales values**.

## Business takeaway

The executive summary identifies the leading product, city, and month in the analyzed sales data. It also records missing values in two fields and inconsistent city names in the original data.
