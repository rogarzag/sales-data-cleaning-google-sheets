## Project Overview

Google Sheets project focused on data cleaning, standardization, KPI analysis, and executive reporting from quarterly sales data.

## Business question

How can a raw quarterly sales dataset be cleaned, standardized, and summarized into reliable commercial KPIs for executive review?

## Approach

The project focused on preparing the data for analysis and building a concise executive summary. The workflow included:

- Cleaning and standardizing raw sales data
- Normalizing text fields and categories
- Calculating key commercial metrics
- Comparing product, city, and monthly performance
- Creating summary views for decision support

## Key findings

- **Tablet** was the best-selling category.
- **Monterrey** was the leading city.
- **November** was the strongest month.

## Business takeaway

A clean and standardized dataset makes it easier to identify the strongest product, market, and seasonal patterns and reduces the risk of drawing conclusions from inconsistent source data.

These findings describe the dataset and provide a starting point for commercial review; they should not be generalized beyond the period and records analyzed.
